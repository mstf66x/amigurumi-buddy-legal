# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 31 July 2026**

This policy explains what Amigurumi Buddy ("the app") collects, why, and what
control you have. It describes the app as it actually behaves — if the app
changes, this page changes with it.

## The short version

- You can use the app **without an account**. The pattern library, your saved
  patterns and your round-by-round progress all work offline, on your device.
- Signing in is optional. It exists so imported patterns can be backed up and
  follow you to another device.
- We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and there is no
  advertising in the app.

## What is stored on your device only

These never leave your phone unless you sign in and use the backup feature:

- Which patterns you saved to **My patterns**, and when you last opened each one.
- Your **round-by-round progress** on every pattern.
- Your **theme and language** preference.
- The **skill level and categories** you picked during onboarding.
- Patterns you imported from a PDF, including the extracted text.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## What we collect if you create an account

Signing in is optional, and you can sign in with an email address, with Apple,
or with Google. In each case we receive:

- A **user ID** issued by Firebase Authentication.
- Your **email address**, when the sign-in method provides one. If you use Sign
  in with Apple and choose *Hide My Email*, we only ever see Apple's relay
  address — we cannot see your real one.
- Your **name**, only if Apple or Google supplies it at first sign-in.

Authentication is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We do not receive
or store your password for Apple or Google sign-in.

## Patterns you import

If you import a pattern PDF while signed in, the **original PDF file** is
uploaded to Firebase Storage in a folder that only your account can read or
write. It is stored so you can restore your library on a new device. We do not
read, index or share these files, and nobody else can access them.

If you are not signed in, imported patterns stay on your device and nothing is
uploaded.

If a pattern cannot be read on-device, the extracted **text** may be sent to our
own server to be structured into steps. Only the text is sent, never the file,
and it is not retained after the response is returned.

## Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand which parts of the app are used, so we
know what to improve. Analytics events deliberately carry **no personal
information** — no names, no email addresses, no pattern contents, no free text.
They record things like which screen was opened and whether an action succeeded.

## Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by **Apple**, and managed through **RevenueCat**,
which tells the app whether your subscription is active. We never see your
payment card or billing details — Apple does not share them with us. RevenueCat
receives an anonymous identifier and your purchase history for this app.

## Who your data is shared with

Only the service providers that make the app work:

| Provider | What they process | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Firebase | Account, imported PDFs, analytics events | Sign-in, backup, analytics |
| RevenueCat | Purchase records, anonymous ID | Subscription status |
| Apple | Payment and subscription | Processing your purchase |

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your choices

- **Use the app without an account** — then nothing is collected at all.
- **Delete your account and data**: email the address below and we will delete
  your account, your uploaded PDFs and any associated records. We complete these
  requests within 30 days.
- **Delete local data**: remove the app from your device.
- Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or
  erase your data, and to object to processing. Contact us and we will help.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect
data from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us
and we will delete it.

## Patterns

The patterns bundled with the app are free patterns published by their
designers. If you are a designer and want your pattern removed, contact us and
we will remove it.

## Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and, where
the change affects you, tell you in the app.
